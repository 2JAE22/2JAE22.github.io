## 🏆 2023 KNU K-Digital Platform Hackathon – Excellent Award(2nd!)

I participated in the **2023 KNU K-Digital Platform Hackathon** and received the  
**Excellent Award** for proposing and developing an AI-based sign language interpretation service titled **“Hand in Hand.”**

This project was highly evaluated for addressing a **real-world social problem**—the communication barrier between hearing-impaired and non-hearing individuals—through practical AI technology.

<br/>

## 📌 Problem Statement
![Proportion_About](..\images\awards\2023-08-30-KNU-Hackton\Proportion_about.png)
The Daegu–Gyeongbuk region has a **relatively high proportion of hearing-impaired individuals**, yet technological support for everyday communication remains limited.

Hearing-impaired people can fully participate in society if communication barriers are removed; however, the availability of sign language interpreters and assistive systems is insufficient in practice.

Our team aimed to address this gap by proposing an **AI-driven, bidirectional communication service** that supports both hearing-impaired and non-hearing users.

<br/>

## 💡 Proposed Solution

The proposed system supports communication in **both directions**:

### 1. Hearing-impaired → Non-hearing
- Input: Sign language video
- Extraction of **key motion points (keypoints)** from hand and facial movements
- Interpretation of sign language sequences into **natural language text** using deep learning models

### 2. Non-hearing → Hearing-impaired
- Input: Spoken language
- Conversion of speech into text using **Speech-to-Text (STT)** technology
- Real-time textual delivery to hearing-impaired users

This design enables **seamless, two-way communication within a single integrated service**.

<br/>

## 🛠️ Technical Overview (High-Level)

- Sign language video analysis based on **keypoint extraction**
- Deep learning–based **temporal sequence modeling**
- Speech-to-Text (STT) for spoken language conversion
- A **web-based prototype** capable of real-time demonstration

> Detailed model architectures and training strategies are documented in the official Hackathon presentation materials.

<br/>

## 🎬 Results and Achievements

- Successfully demonstrated real-time sign language interpretation and speech transcription
- Integrated both communication directions into a single service workflow
- Recognized for **technical feasibility, social impact, and practical applicability**
- Awarded the **Excellent Award** at the 2023 KNU K-Digital Platform Hackathon

<br/>

## 🌱 Expected Impact

- Improved communication efficiency in public institutions and customer service environments
- Expanded social and employment opportunities for hearing-impaired individuals
- Demonstration of AI as a tool for **social problem solving**, beyond purely technical performance
